Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t the camera detect my pose?

Check the Camera permission in iOS Settings and frame your whole body. Light from the front or the side helps tell limbs apart; an elbow or foot out of frame can confuse Vision.

What does the score mean?

It compares detected body landmarks with a pose model. It is not a judge’s mark, a diagnosis or a measure of physical worth.

Where do I find a locked photo?

Open the Journal in the app. Photos are stored locally. If “Also save to Photos” is on, a copy is also added to Photos.

How do I restore my subscription?

In Settings → Subscription, tap “Restore purchases”. Offer details are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.

Contact us

In the app, use Settings → “Something wrong?” to prepare an email with the app version. You can also write directly to apps@dylan-cdo.fr. Only send a photo if you choose to attach it yourself.
