POSELOCK · PRIVACY

Your images stay on your iPhone.

PoseLock analyses the pose on the device. No image, video, pose, body coordinate or score is sent to PoseLock, RevenueCat or TelemetryDeck.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Camera and journal

Vision processes the session live on the iPhone. Locked photos stay in the app’s local storage. If you turn on “Also save to Photos”, a copy is added to the system album. Sharing is always started by you and uses the iPhone’s apps or share sheet.

Subscription

Apple processes the payment. RevenueCat receives the purchase history and subscription status needed to unlock Pro. It receives no photo, video, pose or score.

Email waitlist

If you sign up on this site, we send a link valid for 24 hours to check that the address is yours. It only joins the mailing list after you confirm. By ticking the form’s box and confirming your address, you agree to receive the launch announcement and occasional PoseLock news. This sign-up is independent of your analytics choice.

Resend processes your address to send these emails and, for confirmed addresses, keeps the date and version of your consent and the visit source given in the link that brought you here. We send it no image, pose or score. Unconfirmed requests are not added to the list. Every update will include an unsubscribe link; you can also ask for access to or deletion of your address at apps@dylan-cdo.fr. We keep confirmed addresses until they unsubscribe or ask for deletion; a minimal unsubscribe record may be kept to avoid sending again.

Optional analytics

With your explicit consent, PoseLock sends a few account-free usage events to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service hosted in the European Union.

  • On this site: page views (the page address and, if there is one, the campaign source given in the link), video plays, App Store clicks and waitlist sign-up requests, with the page language.
  • In the app: app opens, onboarding steps completed, paywall views, successful purchases, camera starts, locked photos and shares started, with the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and language.

A random technical identifier, sent only in hashed form, tells consenting visits or installs apart; it is linked neither to your Apple account nor to your subscription. These events do not contain your email address, name, Apple ID, goal, any pose, score, image or video. No ad tracking and no session recording are used.

Without consent, no analytics are sent. You can decline or withdraw consent at any time via “Analytics” at the bottom of this site and in Settings in the app; pending sends are cancelled and the local identifier is erased. Consent previously given to another analytics tool is not carried over. Declining does not limit your use of PoseLock.

Website hosting

This site is hosted by Vercel, which processes the technical data needed to serve the pages, such as IP address and browser.

Support and deletion

A problem report opens Mail with the text you type and the app version; no photo or score is attached automatically. You can erase the journal in Settings. For any question about your data, write to apps@dylan-cdo.fr.
