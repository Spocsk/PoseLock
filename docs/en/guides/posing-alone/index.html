How to practise posing alone

Without a partner, the problem isn’t just triggering a photo: you need to know whether the line holds before you leave the position.

A repeatable setup

  1. Steady the iPhone and clear enough space to show your whole body.
  2. Choose a background and lighting that clearly separate your arms and legs from the room.
  3. Mark your spot on the floor so you find the same distance every attempt.
  4. Repeat one pose for a few takes, then compare the latest with an earlier one.

Avoid three common mistakes

Changing the viewing angle. A different viewpoint can create an apparent difference that isn’t progress. Cutting off hands and feet. Hands or feet out of frame make the landmarks less reliable. Comparing different poses. Keep the same pose and similar conditions before judging any change.
